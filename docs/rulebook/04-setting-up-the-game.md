# Setting Up the Game

> Source: *Star Trek* Customizable Card Game Second Edition Rulebook, version 4,
> April 2015, The Continuing Committee, pages 6–7,
> <https://www.trekcc.org/op/rulebook.pdf>. Verbatim transcription; see
> [README.md](README.md) for the conventions. Later rulings of The Continuing
> Committee can override this text.

<!-- page 6 -->

Take all five of your mission cards and place them face up in a row on the table
in front of you. You may place them in any order you choose. Shuffle your dilemma
pile and place it off to your left. Shuffle your deck and place it off to your
right. (See a sample layout on the next page.)

Determine randomly who goes first. Draw seven cards from your deck to form your
opening hand.

Make sure to remember who takes the first turn, as it may be important later in
the game.

<!-- page 7 -->

## Sample layout

*Page 7 is a picture of a two-player table, printed sideways, with these labels.*

Your side of the table, seen from your seat:

- **Your Missions**: a row of five missions in the middle of the table.
- **Your Personnel & Ships**: between you and your missions, in line with the
  mission each one is at.
- Along your edge of the table, from left to right: **Your Dilemma Pile**,
  **Your Brig** (a captive personnel), **Your Core** (events), and **Your Deck &
  Discard Pile**.

Your opponent's side mirrors yours across the table: **Opponent's Missions** in
a row facing yours, **Opponent's Personnel & Ships**, and along the far edge
**Opponent's Dilemma Pile**, **Opponent's Brig**, **Opponent's Core**, and
**Opponent's Deck & Discard Pile**, each to the opponent's own left or right.
