# 3. Discard Excess Cards

> Source: *Star Trek* Customizable Card Game Second Edition Rulebook, version 4,
> April 2015, The Continuing Committee, page 13,
> <https://www.trekcc.org/op/rulebook.pdf>. Verbatim transcription; see
> [README.md](README.md) for the conventions. Later rulings of The Continuing
> Committee can override this text.

The third and last segment of the turn. See [Playing the
Game](05-playing-the-game.md) for the turn sequence.

<!-- page 13 -->

During this segment of your turn, you may be required to discard cards from your
hand. If you have more than seven cards in your hand, you must choose and discard
cards until you have only seven.

Next, you may complete any actions that happen "at the end of each of your
turns." Each of these actions may be performed only once per turn. You may
perform them in any order.

Once you have done these things, your turn ends. At this time, all stopped cards
commanded by all players become unstopped, and all ships commanded by all players
have their full Range restored.
