<!-- page 8 -->

Each player, going clockwise around the table, takes a turn comprised of the
following sequence of turn segments.

1. Play and Draw Cards
2. Execute Orders
3. Discard Excess Cards

When one player finishes his or her turn, the next player in clockwise rotation
(to the left) takes a turn, and so on.

## Turn Sequence

When your turn begins, you may complete any actions that happen "at the start of
each of your turns." Each of these actions may be performed only once per turn.
You may perform them in any order.

The three segments each have a file of their own:

1. [Play and Draw Cards](06-play-and-draw-cards.md)
2. [Execute Orders](07-execute-orders.md)
3. [Discard Excess Cards](08-discard-excess-cards.md)
